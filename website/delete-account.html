Account Management

Delete Your Account

You can delete your Servix account at any time, directly from the app or by contacting us below.

⚠️

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your profile, messages, and booking history cannot be recovered. Make sure you have saved any information you need before proceeding.

How to Delete from the App

The fastest way is directly inside the Servix app. Follow these steps:

  1. 1 Open the Servix app and sign in to your account.
  2. 2 Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. 3 Scroll down to the Account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. 4 Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your account will be permanently removed.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number deleted
  • Your profile photos and bio deleted
  • Your chat messages and booking history deleted
  • Your verification documents (passport / national ID / selfie) deleted
  • Your payment and subscription records deleted

Anonymised, aggregated statistics (e.g. total number of bookings per area) may be retained for internal analytics but cannot be linked back to you.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to sign in to the app to delete your account, send us an email from the address registered to your Servix account and we will delete it manually within 7 business days.

📧 Email us at: support@servix.world

Subject line: Account Deletion Request

Include your registered email address so we can locate your account.
